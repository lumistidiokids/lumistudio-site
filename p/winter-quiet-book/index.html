Winter Quiet Book

Printable busy book with 12 velcro activities, ages 2-5

Buy in our shop

This winter quiet book printable keeps toddlers busy with 12 hands-on velcro activities: matching, sorting, counting and puzzles, with no screens and no mess.

What is inside

  • 26 pages: cover, how to use, contents
  • 12 activities, each with its own pieces page: shadow match, mitten pairs, decorate the tree, build a snowman, feed the animals, winter or summer, big or small, small medium big, counting 1 to 5, 2 puzzles, trace the path

Skills

  • Fine motor, matching, colors, sorting, sizes and counting. Quiet play for home, travel and preschool.

How to use

  • Print on cardstock, laminate, cut out the pieces and add velcro dots. Each piece fits its place exactly. Velcro and laminator not included. Always supervise young children.

Files

  • 1 PDF US Letter (8.5x11 in) and 1 PDF A4, 300 DPI
  • Digital download, nothing will be shipped
  • Colors may look a little different depending on your screen and printer
