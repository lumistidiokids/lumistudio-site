Halloween ABC Flashcards

26 cute, not scary alphabet cards with ABC poster, ages 2-5

Many Halloween pictures are too scary for little kids, and plain alphabet cards get boring fast. These ABC cards have big clear letters and a cute, friendly Halloween picture for every letter, so learning the alphabet feels like a party.

What is inside

  • 26 flashcards from A to Z, 2 per page, with big upper and lower case letters: apple, bat, cauldron, dragon, elf, frog, ghost, haunted house, insect, jack-o-lantern, kitten, lantern, mummy, night, owl, pumpkin, queen, raven, skeleton, treats, unicorn, vampire, witch, x-ray, yarn, zombie
  • Soft watercolor pictures, cute and not scary
  • ABC poster with all 26 letters, portrait and landscape
  • Cards in landscape and portrait layout

How to use

  • Print on cardstock, laminate if you like, and cut
  • Say the letter and the word, find the sound together
  • Play memory or "find the letter" games
  • Hang the poster on the wall or fridge

Files

  • 2 PDF in US Letter: cards with 2 posters, cards in portrait layout
  • 2 PDF in A4: cards with 2 posters, cards in portrait layout
