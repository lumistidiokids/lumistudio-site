My First Winter Words Flashcards

26 winter picture cards with action prompts, ages 1-4

Buy in our shop

Instant digital download. Nothing will be shipped.

Toddlers do not learn new words by sitting still. Every card here has a picture, a word and one simple action, so your child can say the word and move at the same time.

What is inside

  • 26 flashcards, 2 per page: snowflake, snowball, snowman, sled, hat, scarf, mittens, boots, bear, penguin, rabbit, cookie, cocoa, lantern, tree, snow globe, bell, star, gift, ribbon, balloon, music, crown, clock, wish, happy new year
  • A simple action on every card: stamp your feet, waddle like a penguin, hop like a bunny
  • Word poster with all 26 words, portrait and landscape

How to use

  • Print on cardstock, laminate if you like, and cut
  • Show a card, say the word together, then do the action
  • Hang the poster on the wall or fridge

Files

  • 1 PDF in US Letter: 13 card pages + 2 posters
  • 1 PDF in A4: 13 card pages + 2 posters
  • Instant digital download, nothing will be shipped. Colors may look a little different depending on your screen and printer.
