Snowman Pancakes for Toddlers: An Easy Winter Breakfast

Snowman pancakes turn an ordinary breakfast into a little winter party. You need three pancakes, a few berries and about 15 minutes. Your toddler can help decorate, and you will get a happy "Look, a snowman!" at the table.

What you need

  • Your usual pancake batter (homemade or a mix)
  • A handful of blueberries
  • 2 or 3 strawberries
  • A small piece of soft cooked carrot or a thin slice of mango for the nose
  • Optional: a light dusting of powdered sugar or a spoon of yogurt for "snow"

How to make snowman pancakes

  1. Cook three sizes. A grown-up makes one small, one medium and one big pancake. The hot pan is a grown-up job; little helpers can watch from a safe distance.
  2. Build the snowman. Lay the pancakes in a row on a plate: small for the head, medium for the body, big for the bottom.
  3. Add eyes and buttons. Use blueberry pieces for two eyes and three buttons on each of the bigger pancakes.
  4. Give him a nose. A tiny triangle of soft cooked carrot or mango makes a perfect snowman nose.
  5. Wrap the scarf. Place strawberry pieces between the head and the body like a red scarf.

Toddler safety tips

Small round foods are a choking risk for young children. A few simple steps keep this breakfast safe:

  • Cut blueberries into quarters or gently squash them for toddlers. Do not serve them whole to little ones.
  • Use soft foods for the nose. Raw carrot is hard and can be a choking risk, so use soft cooked carrot or ripe mango.
  • Cut strawberries into small pieces and pancakes into bite-size strips when it is time to eat.
  • Sit down to eat. Children should eat sitting at the table with a grown-up nearby, not while walking or playing.

Let your toddler help

Decorating is the fun part, and it is great practice for little hands. Put the cut fruit in small bowls and let your child place the eyes, buttons and scarf. Talk while you build: "Which pancake is the biggest? How many buttons do we need? What color is the scarf?" You are practicing sizes, counting and colors without any worksheet at all.

More winter breakfast ideas

  • Snowman toast: three round toast circles with a little cream cheese.
  • Snowy oatmeal: a spoon of yogurt "snow" with berry pieces on top.
  • Mitten pancakes: one oval pancake with a small thumb on the side.
