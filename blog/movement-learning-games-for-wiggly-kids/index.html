12 Movement Learning Games for Wiggly Kids

Some children learn best when they are moving. If your child will not sit still for a worksheet, bring the learning to their feet. These 12 games need no special supplies and work for ages 2 to 5.

Letter games

  1. Letter hop. Write 3 letters on paper and put them on the floor. Call a letter, your child hops to it.
  2. Sound stomp. Say words. If a word starts with the letter of the week, your child stomps. If not, they freeze.
  3. Body letters. Make letters with your bodies: arms up for Y, lie straight for I, make a circle for O.
  4. Animal walk ABC. B for bear walk, C for crab walk, F for frog jumps.

Number games

  1. Jump and count. Roll a die or show a number card. Jump that many times and count out loud.
  2. Clap patterns. Clap 2, then 3, then 4. Your child copies and counts.
  3. Step counting. Count the steps from the door to the kitchen. Then try giant steps and tiny steps.
  4. Number freeze dance. Dance to music. When it stops, show fingers for a number and your child copies.

Colors and shapes

  1. Color run. Call a color, your child runs to touch something in that color.
  2. Shape hunt. Find 3 circles in the room: a plate, a clock, a button.
  3. Tape shapes. Make a big square, circle and triangle with tape on the floor. Walk along the lines.
  4. Simon says shapes. "Simon says draw a triangle in the air."

Tips for success

  • Play for 5 to 10 minutes, then stop while it is still fun.
  • Mix a movement game with a short quiet page: move, sit, move.
  • Let your child be the leader sometimes. Kids love giving the instructions.
