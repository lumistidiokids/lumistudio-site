20 Screen-Free Activities for Toddlers at Home

Screens are part of family life, and that is okay. But many parents want more screen-free time and need ideas that work on a busy day. Health experts such as the American Academy of Pediatrics suggest limiting screen time for children ages 2 to 5 to about one hour a day of high-quality programs, ideally watched together. Here are 20 simple things to do instead.

Quiet activities

  1. Coloring pages with big, simple pictures
  2. Sticker dots on a printed letter or shape
  3. Play dough snakes, balls and letters
  4. Sorting buttons, pasta or blocks by color
  5. Reading the same picture book again (repetition is great)
  6. Threading big beads or pasta on a string
  7. Puzzles with a few large pieces

Active activities

  1. Freeze dance
  2. Pillow obstacle course
  3. Animal walks: bear, frog, crab
  4. Balloon keep-it-up
  5. Indoor treasure hunt for "something red" or "something round"
  6. Bubble popping and counting

Learning activities

  1. Letter of the day hunt in books and on boxes
  2. Counting snacks before eating them
  3. Tracing big dotted letters with a finger, then a crayon
  4. Spot the difference pictures
  5. Matching socks: big and small, same and different
  6. Helping in the kitchen: washing, stirring, counting spoons
  7. A short daily learning page with a grown-up

Make it easy for yourself

The hardest part of screen-free time is often thinking of what to do. Keep a small box of ready printables, crayons and stickers. When the day gets hard, open the box instead of the tablet.
