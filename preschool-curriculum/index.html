Questions parents ask

How much time does it take each day?

About 15 minutes: two or three short pages and a quick movement game. Stop while it is still fun.

What age is it for?

The monthly curriculum is made for 3 year olds. Our books, coloring and feelings sets work for ages 2 to 5.

Do I need special supplies?

No. A printer, crayons and sometimes glue or stickers. There are no scissors tasks for your child.

Is it aligned with standards?

Skills are checked against the Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework (ELOF), with the level based on CDC milestones. Lumi Studio is not affiliated with Head Start or the CDC.

What paper size do I get?

Every pack comes in US Letter and A4 PDF. It is an instant digital download, nothing is shipped.

Can I use it in my classroom or daycare?

Yes, for your own class. Please do not share or resell the files.
