Waiting Room Games for Kids Ages 2-5

The doctor is running late, your bag is empty and your toddler is getting wiggly. These 10 quiet games need nothing but you. Print the list, fold it into your bag or save it on your phone for the next appointment.

  1. I Spy. Take turns: I spy something blue. Older kids can try shapes or letters.
  2. Count It. How many chairs are in the room? How many people wear glasses? Count together in a whisper.
  3. Finger Friends. Your fingers become characters. They say hello, have a little adventure and go to sleep in your pocket.
  4. Tiny Simon Says. Simon Says with small, quiet moves only: wiggle your nose, blink twice, tap your knee.
  5. Guess the Animal. Give clues one by one: I live on a farm, I say moo. Your child guesses. Then switch.
  6. Draw on My Hand. Trace a shape or letter on your child's palm with your finger. Can they guess it with their eyes closed?
  7. Shape Hunt. Look for circles in the room: a clock, a button, a light. Then squares, then triangles.
  8. Whisper Echo. Whisper a funny word. Your child whispers it back. Make the words longer and sillier.
  9. Story Chain. Start a story with one sentence. Your child adds the next one. Take turns until the story ends.
  10. Quiet Stretch. Stretch up tall like a tree, then fold small like a seed. Slow stretching helps a wiggly body calm down.

Tips that make it work

  • Pack a tiny waiting kit: a crayon, a small notebook and a printed puzzle page.
  • Whispering games keep the volume low and feel a little special.
  • Praise the waiting: You waited so patiently, that was hard work!
