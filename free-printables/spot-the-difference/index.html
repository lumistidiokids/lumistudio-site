How to play

  1. Look at the top picture first and talk about what you see.
  2. Now compare it with the bottom picture. What changed? A color, a shape, or something missing?
  3. Point to each difference or circle it on the printed page.
  4. Stuck? Open the answers under each puzzle.

Why spot the difference is good for little kids

Looking for differences asks a child to look closely, compare two pictures and keep going until the job is done. It is a calm, screen-free way to practice attention to detail, and it is perfect for waiting rooms, restaurants and car rides. Talking about the pictures together also builds new words.

Tips for every age

  • For a 3 year old, point to one area at a time: Look at the sky. Is something missing?
  • Missing objects are easier than tiny changes, so start with these.
  • Celebrate every find. If your child gets stuck, give a clue instead of the answer.
