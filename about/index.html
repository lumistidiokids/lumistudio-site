About

Learning that feels like play

Lumi Studio creates printable learning activities for toddlers and preschoolers ages 2-5. Every page is designed to keep little ones curious, busy and proud of what they can do.

Why parents choose us

  • No planning needed. Each month is ready to print: open the page, sit down for 15 minutes, done.
  • Checked against standards. Skills are checked against Head Start ELOF goals, with the level based on CDC milestones, so your child learns the right skills at the right age.
  • Less screen time. Real paper, crayons and conversation instead of another app.
  • Made for wiggly kids. If your child will not sit still, every set includes movement games, hands-on tasks and play ideas, so learning happens on the floor, at the table or on the go.

Many ways to learn

  • Tracing and fine motor pages
  • Coloring and big, simple pictures
  • Counting, shapes and colors
  • Movement and action games: stomp, clap, hop
  • Parent and child games with no extra supplies

All products are instant digital downloads in US Letter and A4. Nothing will be shipped.

Lumi Studio is not affiliated with Head Start or the CDC.
