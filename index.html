Questions

Good to know

Who is Lumi Studio for?

Families with toddlers and kids: parents, grandparents, homeschoolers and teachers. Our preschool curriculum is made for age 3, coloring pages, games and cards work for older kids too, and creators will find clipart and invitations for their own projects.

How do I get my files?

Everything is an instant digital download. You get the files right after checkout, nothing is shipped.

What paper size do I get?

Our printables come in US Letter, and most sets also in A4. Print at home or at a copy shop.

Do I need special supplies?

No. A printer, crayons and sometimes glue or stickers. There are no scissors tasks for young children.

Is the preschool curriculum aligned with standards?

Skills are checked against the Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework (ELOF), with the level based on CDC milestones. Lumi Studio is not affiliated with Head Start or the CDC.

Can I use it in my classroom or daycare?

Yes, for your own class. Please do not share or resell the files. Clipart comes with its own license.
